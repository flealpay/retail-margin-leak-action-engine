# Retail Margin Leak & Action Engine

Language used to distinguish observed financial changes, synthetic mechanisms and proposed commercial decisions.

## Language

**Margin erosion**:
A decline in gross profit as a share of sales after discounts. It can occur even when total gross profit grows.
_Avoid_: Net-profit loss, cash loss

**Adverse contribution**:
A negative allocated component of the observed gross-profit change. Its size is neither the net company loss nor an amount proven recoverable.
_Avoid_: Recoverable leakage, causal impact

**Joint mix**:
The distribution of sold units across SKU, region and channel together.
_Avoid_: Product mix alone, customer mix

**Action priority**:
A proposed commercial investigation or controlled test, with an explanation and stated assumptions.
_Avoid_: Executed decision, guaranteed improvement

**Fixed-volume scenario**:
A conditional gross-profit calculation that changes specified unit economics while holding sold units constant.
_Avoid_: Recovery forecast, incremental uplift

**Planted mechanism**:
A deliberately introduced business pattern in the synthetic environment, known separately from the analysis findings.
_Avoid_: Discovered real-world cause

**Snapshot availability**:
The proportion of observed inventory snapshots showing stock in the stated product, location and time scope.
_Avoid_: Intraday availability, lost demand
