Sales are growing. Why is profit falling?

I built a retail analytics project using 12 months of synthetic data covering 500 products and 180,000 sales lines.

Revenue grew 6.5%, while gross profit fell 2.7%.

Using SQL and Python, I separated the effects of volume, sales mix, prices, discounts and costs. Discounting was the biggest negative driver.

The proposed fix: reduce discounts by up to six percentage points on flagged offers—for example, from 20% to 14%.

At unchanged sales volume, this added €112k in modeled gross profit, enough to close the €106k gap.

This is a scenario result. Customers may buy less when discounts shrink, so the next step would be a controlled test measuring both units sold and gross profit.
